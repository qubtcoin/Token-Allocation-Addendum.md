## Background
To reduce single‑address large‑balance exposure, improve on‑chain transparency and mitigate perceived centralization risks, token reserves belonging to the Qubtcoin Foundation, Quantum Computing Foundation and Team Reserve have been split across multiple on‑chain addresses.

During the Solana Superposition Fair‑Launch phase, ecosystem‑targeted airdrops will be executed to expand early ecosystem collaboration and project influence.
This addendum also clarifies boundaries and risk disclosures for secondary‑market activity during the Solana‑hosted phase.

## Core Rules
1. Total token supply and percentage allocations remain fully consistent with V1.0 whitepaper. No minting, burning or re‑allocation has occurred.
2. After splitting, **no single on‑chain address holds more than 10% of total token supply**.
3. Lock‑up periods and Energy‑Well release rules are strictly enforced:
    - Qubtcoin Foundation: 45.24% total, 5‑year lock‑up, subject to Energy‑Well release logic
    - Quantum Computing Foundation: 20.00% total, 5‑year lock‑up, subject to Energy‑Well release logic
    - Team Reserve: 15.00% total, 2‑year lock‑up
    - Turing Award Foundation: 9.76%, released for research grants
    - Community / Liquidity Reserve: 10.00%, for liquidity, node incentives and Solana‑phase ecosystem airdrops
4. All split reserve addresses are managed via project multi‑sig. **No single‑signer wallet controls reserve assets**. Upon completion of the Great‑Collapse mainnet launch, all reserves will be transferred to foundation multi‑sig governance.
5. Address‑splitting is purely an on‑chain presentation measure and does not change beneficial ownership or release schedules. Regulators will apply combined‑balance penetration review across all controlled addresses.

### Special Note on Team Reserve
The 15.00% Team Reserve defined in V1.0 whitepaper functions **as a future‑oriented reserve pool for global core developers**, and is not reserved exclusively for the current initiator.

Like Bitcoin launched by Satoshi Nakamoto, Qubtcoin is presently advanced by a single builder conducting protocol research, whitepaper drafting and prototype development. A full core‑developer team has not yet been assembled.

For cleaner on‑chain visual presentation, the 15.00% Team Reserve is divided across multiple addresses. **At present these split addresses remain under multi‑sig control; no tokens have been actually distributed to any individual or external party.**

As the project matures, open recruitment will target specialists in quantum cryptography, quantum hardware and protocol engineering. Each real‑world distribution to newly‑onboarded developers will update this addendum with full on‑chain address audit trail.

Team‑reserve lock‑up rules remain unchanged: 2‑year lock‑up; tokens cannot be traded or released before unlocking conditions are satisfied.

### Solana Fair‑Launch Airdrop Statement (Superposition Phase)
During the Solana‑based Superposition phase (Meme‑era experimental credentials), targeted ecosystem airdrops will be delivered to Solana‑ecosystem market‑makers, liquidity providers, protocol builders and reputable collaborating projects.

1. Source of airdrop tokens: **Drawn entirely from the 10.00% Community / Liquidity Reserve pool. Total airdrop size approx. 1 500 000 tokens. No tokens are taken from Qubtcoin Foundation, Quantum Computing Foundation or locked Team Reserve.**
2. These 1,500,000 tokens are permanently disbursed for Superposition‑phase ecosystem airdrop and will NOT be returned to the Community / Liquidity Reserve pool.
3. Scope: Multiple recipient addresses, with standard allotments and enhanced allotments for high‑impact ecosystem partners; total airdrop volume approx. 1 500 000 tokens.
4. Critical legal & narrative boundaries:
    - Tokens on Solana are **Superposition‑phase experimental credentials only, Meme‑era assets confined to Solana**.
    - **This airdrop does NOT constitute a 1:1 conversion guarantee for future Q‑Grid mainnet tokens. Mainnet launch carries substantial hardware‑engineering and R&D uncertainty and is not assured.**
    - This airdrop is not an investment solicitation. Recipients accept full experimental‑project risk including total loss of value. Airdrop receipt does not represent any exchange‑listing commitment.
5. Transparency requirements:
    - Full recipient addresses and token quantities will be appended to this document upon Great‑Collapse native mainnet launch.
    - This is targeted ecosystem collaboration airdrop, not broad retail user giveaway.
    - Airdropped assets are subject to phase‑mapping logic and Energy‑Well constraints defined in the whitepaper.

> Important reminder: Receiving airdrop tokens does not guarantee future rights to native Q‑Grid tokens. All mapping rules will be published near mainnet launch. The project cannot control post‑airdrop on‑chain behaviour including recipient sell‑offs.
